# Balance report

Generated by the bot (`sim/bot.js`), a one-ply heuristic that plays like a focused casual player: it tries every legal column, keeps the drop that merges most and stacks least, and spends a swipe when the bar is full and the swipe helps. Re-run after changing `levels.json`, the unlock thresholds, or the spawn weights in `BoardLogic`:

```
node sim/balance.js --runs 100 --endless 200
```

The bot does **not** model special tiles, the swapper, merger, wildcard or frenzy. Treat every number here as a floor for a competent human, and as a baseline for "is this player struggling more than expected" rather than as a target.

### Power-up affordability

Merges are the currency. Measured against gross merges earned across a
game, so these are ceilings - a player spending on swipes has less.
